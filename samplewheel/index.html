WHAT IT DOES

Sample video, not just sound

Drop in a video and every pad you hit plays its part of the sound and the picture with it, frame for frame.

Twelve pads on a wheel

Chop by beats, by hits or evenly, then drag any chop's edges on the waveform. Each pad has its own pitch and level.

Four ways to play

One-shot, Gate, Repeat and Grain, with the dials each one needs right under the switch.

Locked to your tempo

Warp the sample to your project's tempo without changing its pitch. Pads play from MIDI, your keyboard or a click.

Start on your phone

Sample a video in Dronewheel, then open the same project in the plugin. Chops, settings and gain stay in sync both ways.

Your phone loops, in the DAW

Loops you record in SampleWheel on the phone show up as clips you can step through and play on your DAW's grid.

Make a project here, finish it there

Turn the sample in the plugin into a new Dronewheel project and carry on with it on your phone.

Performance mode

The video fills the window with the wheel over it, for playing live or recording a take.
