Support

Email support@wheelverseaudio.com and say which app and device you are on. You will hear back from a person.

DRONEWHEEL

I paid for Pro but it is locked.Open the Pro screen in the app (any locked sound or feature opens it) and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the account you bought it on.

My projects are not on my other phone.Sign in to the same account on both. Projects sync when the app is open and online.

How do I cancel Pro?Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google: in your phone's Settings, under subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?In the app's settings, choose Delete account. Your projects in the cloud go with it.

SAMPLEWHEEL PLUGIN

How do I unlock it?Sign in to the plugin with your Dronewheel account. It unlocks with Dronewheel Pro, or with a purchase made with that account's email.

My DAW does not see it.The plugin installs to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Rescan your plugins, or restart the DAW.

A phone video shows no picture.Wait for the progress in the middle of the wheel to finish: the first load of a video decodes its picture, later loads are instant.
