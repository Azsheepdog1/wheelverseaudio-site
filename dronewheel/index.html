DRONEWHEEL

A whole music studio on one phone.

Drones, drums, samples, DJ decks and your own voice, played live from one wheel. No laptop required.

App Store Google Play

WHAT IT DOES

Play in any key

Twelve notes round a wheel, in the key and scale you choose. Hold a note to drone it, or play chords.

Drums that keep time

A kit on its own wheel, with grooves that lock to the project's tempo.

Sample anything

Chop a sound or a video onto twelve pads and play it back across the wheel.

Your own voice

Loop the mic, harmonize it and put effects on it as you play.

Two DJ decks

Mix songs with tempo sync, split them into stems and loop parts of them.

Loops, a mixer and a master

Record loops into clips, launch them, mix every deck and record the whole thing.

On all your devices

Projects sync through your account, so a project on one phone is on the other.

Continue in your DAW

Open a project's SampleWheel in the SampleWheel plugin on your computer.

DRONEWHEEL PRO

The full studio, and the plugin with it.

Pro opens every sound, mode and deck in the app, and unlocks the SampleWheel plugin on your computer with the same account.
